## Chosen Direction: Champagne Editorial

### Design Movement

The site follows **contemporary fashion editorial design**, combining the compositional confidence of a luxury beauty campaign with the clarity of a high-converting local service website. It will never imitate a generic salon template; the composition treats each section as a deliberate magazine spread with large images, expressive typography, and asymmetrical image framing.

### Core Principles

1. **Photography sets the emotional temperature.** Large, varied beauty and bridal images establish aspiration before the visitor reads the service copy.
2. **Luxury comes from restraint.** The interface uses a small palette, quiet borders, precise spacing, and a few deliberate metallic accents instead of excessive decoration.
3. **Conversion stays effortless.** The navigation, desktop CTA, mobile bottom bar, service cards, and final panel all route directly to WhatsApp or phone with no booking friction.
4. **Editorial rhythm replaces a uniform card grid.** Spacious type-led sections alternate with visual collages and gently varied image proportions.

### Color Philosophy

Warm ivory creates a soft premium canvas and complements skin tones in salon photography. Near-black brings the necessary contrast and authority, while a muted champagne accent provides a subtle, ownable signal of occasion and bridal refinement. Rose is used sparingly for gentle feminine emphasis, never as a dominant sugary background.

### Layout Paradigm

The site is a **vertical editorial journey** rather than a centralized card dashboard. It uses full-bleed imagery, staggered two-column moments, an asymmetric service mosaic, and a gallery that visually loosens as the visitor moves down the page. On mobile, the layout becomes a deliberate sequence of image, message, and clear action—not a squeezed desktop grid.

### Signature Elements

1. A small champagne **location ribbon** on the hero and select cards that anchors the salon in Islamabad.
2. Thin **editorial rule lines** and finely spaced uppercase labels to create a beauty-magazine rhythm.
3. A muted **champagne halo** behind selective images and the final booking panel, used as a recurring visual signature.

### Interaction Philosophy

Every interaction should communicate confidence and ease. Buttons give a small physical press response, navigation scrolls smoothly to anchored sections, service booking opens a prepared WhatsApp conversation, and the mobile bar stays available without obscuring content. Movement is responsive and quiet, never theatrical.

### Animation

Use 180–260ms custom ease-out transitions for buttons, links, and image hovers. Image tiles may fade upward by 10–14px during initial scrolling, staggered in short sequences. Use only `transform` and `opacity`, and disable non-essential movement under `prefers-reduced-motion`. There are no looping decorative animations, parallax effects, or slow entrance sequences.

### Typography System

**Playfair Display** is the expressive display face for H1, H2, and restrained italic emphasis. **Manrope** is used for navigation, buttons, compact labels, addresses, and body copy for cleaner mobile readability than a second decorative serif. **Great Vibes** may appear only as a short bridal accent, never as critical navigation or body copy. The wordmark uses a custom letter-spaced treatment rather than a default font lockup.

### Brand Essence

**Fab's Salon & Bridal Lounge is Islamabad's photography-led beauty destination for clients seeking an elevated, direct-to-stylist salon and bridal enquiry experience.**

**Personality:** poised, warm, polished.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are calm, evocative, and assured; CTAs are clear and direct; microcopy explains what happens next without over-promising confirmation or availability. Avoid generic filler and unsupported superlatives.

Example headline: **Beauty, Bridal & You — Elevated.**

Example CTA support: **Start your enquiry on WhatsApp. The Fab's team will share available timings and details.**

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo concept is a bold, text-free **interlocking F and petal silhouette**, with a single elegant cut through the mark that recalls a salon mirror arc. The header pairs this symbol with a custom spaced `FAB'S` wordmark in a refined high-contrast serif treatment. The generated symbol is used prominently in the header and as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color

**Champagne Veil — #B58B58.** A warm, muted metallic that distinguishes Fab's from generic pink salon sites while complementing ivory backgrounds and bridal photography.

## Style Decisions

- Photography will be unified with warm champagne light, close-crop beauty or bridal focus, and a subtle editorial grade. Clinical interiors, generic product flatlays, and unrelated lifestyle imagery are excluded unless visually harmonized into this world.
- The interlocking F/petal monogram is a recurring editorial signature: present in the header, as a quiet hero watermark, and within the lower-page booking moment.
- Champagne Veil `#B58B58` remains a restrained metallic signal reserved for fine rules, location ribbons, monogram moments, key numerals, and primary action emphasis.
